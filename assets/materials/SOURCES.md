# Environment surfaces

The local textures are WebP conversions of Poly Haven scans, distributed under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The 1024-pixel plaster tile layers two scans to vary the exposed brick. Most other colour maps are 512 pixels. Corrugated steel uses a 256-pixel OpenGL normal map. The road paint combines asphalt grain with generated paint wear.
